About the club

Mission

Why this club exists

As parents to a child with autism, we understand firsthand how difficult it can be to find recreational opportunities where children with different abilities feel truly welcomed, supported, and included. What began as a desire to create a place where our son Bennett could create the same friendships our older daughter, Hanna experiences within her club sports has grown into a mission to provide those same opportunities to other families in our community.

All Abilities Sports Club

What the club does

All Abilities Sports Club is a nonprofit organization dedicated to providing inclusive sports opportunities for children of all abilities in the Dayton, Ohio area. Through adaptive programming, supportive coaching and a welcoming community, we help every child build confidence, friendships and a lifelong love of sports.

The first program is Winter Indoor Soccer in Beavercreek and the Dayton area. Basketball, baseball, and flag football come later.
