Privacy

How this website handles information.

A plain-language notice for families. It describes this website only.

Who we are

All Abilities Sports Club is a nonprofit organization in Beavercreek and the Dayton, Ohio area. Website questions can go to amber.davidson@allabilitiessportsclub.com.

What this site collects

This is a static website. It does not ask you to create an account. Sign-up and contact forms do not save messages on the site. They open your email app so you can send what you typed to the club.

If you email the club, the message includes the fields you filled in, such as your name, email, phone if you added one, an athlete’s first name, age, volunteer role, and your note. Please do not send medical records through the form.

Children

A parent, guardian, or adult volunteer should be the person who writes to the club. The athlete form asks for a first name only. The club does not knowingly collect information directly from children through this website.

Donations

The club plans to use Zeffy for donations. The Zeffy form is not connected on this site yet, so this website does not send payment details anywhere. When a Zeffy form is added, Zeffy’s own privacy practices will cover gifts made there.

What stays on your device

The home page can play a short intro once each visit. A flag in session storage remembers that you already saw it, or that you skipped it. The flag is cleared when the browsing session ends. The site does not use that flag to identify you.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. This website does not run advertising cookies, and it does not include a shop.

Photos

The site uses the club logo, a sponsorship flyer, and drawn sports graphics. It does not publish identifiable photos of children.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted on this page. This version is dated November 4, 2026.
