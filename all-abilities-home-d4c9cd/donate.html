Sponsors

Partner with the club.

All Abilities Sports Club is a nonprofit organization dedicated to providing inclusive sports opportunities for children of all abilities in the Dayton, Ohio area. Sponsorship levels below are from the club’s flyer.

$500

Event Sponsor

Helps provide opportunities to host special events and additional activities for the children we serve, such as sensory-friendly events, family activities, skills clinics, and community gatherings outside of our regular sports programs.

Recognition includes

  • Recognition on social media
  • Recognition at club events
  • Shown as an Event Sponsor on our website
  • A thank-you for supporting inclusive opportunities

$1,000

Facility Sponsor

Supports the spaces where athletes of all abilities learn, play, and belong.

Recognition includes

  • Recognition on social media
  • Recognition on our website
  • Acknowledgment in program materials
  • Featured as a Facility Sponsor
  • A thank-you for helping provide a safe and inclusive space

$2,000

Program Sponsor

Directly supports our inclusive sports programs, coaching, adaptive equipment, and affordable participation.

Recognition includes

  • Recognition on social media and our website
  • Acknowledgment in all program communications
  • Featured as a Program Sponsor
  • Invitation to a special program event
  • A thank-you for investing in every athlete

$7,500+

Founding Sponsor

Be a founding partner and help shape the future of inclusive sports in our community.

Recognition includes

  • Premier name and logo placement on most marketing materials
  • Featured on our website and social media
  • Recognition in press releases and community outreach
  • Invitation to exclusive events and program experiences
  • Opportunities for customized recognition and partnership benefits
