Winter Indoor Soccer

Kids run, kick, and score together at their own pace. No experience is required. Families in Beavercreek and the Dayton, Ohio area are invited.

Registration opens
November 20, 2026
Registration closes
When 48 spots are filled, or December 18, 2026, whichever comes first
Fee
$30 per participant
Sessions
Sundays, January 10 through February 14, 2027
Length
6 weeks
Where
Beavercreek and the Dayton, Ohio area. A street address is not listed on this site yet.

How to join

  1. 1 Send a note from the sign-up page. That email does not reserve a spot or take payment.
  2. 2 Registration opens November 20, 2026. The fee is $30 per participant, paid with the club, not on this website.
  3. 3 Spots stop at 48 athletes, or on December 18, 2026.
