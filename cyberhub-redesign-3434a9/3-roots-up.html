FAQ

Straight answers.

Is it really one price, with nothing monthly?

Yes. You pay the flat price once: 50% to start and 50% at launch, before the site goes live. We don't sell monthly plans. Your site is hosted free on GitHub Pages, so your only ongoing cost is your own domain renewal.

Who owns the website?

You do. Your domain and hosting are in your name from day one. When you pay the final invoice, ownership of the custom design and code transfers to you under our short written agreement. Stock photos and fonts stay under their own free licenses, which cover your use.

How do you get access to my accounts?

We never ask for your passwords. You invite us instead, for example as a Manager on your Google Business Profile. At hand-off you hold every login, you remove our access, and we show you how.

What is the 90-day fix window?

For 90 days after launch, we fix anything that isn't working or isn't what we agreed, free. After that, small updates are $50 each and bigger work gets a new written quote, with no retainer.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes. We set up a simple editor so you can change text, photos and events yourself, and we show you how. Design and layout changes come back to us.

What do you need from me?

Your current web address, your logo if you have one, any photos you'd like used, and a few minutes to confirm hours, services and prices. We can use free-license stock photos where you don't have your own.

Do you only work in the Dayton area?

We're based in the Dayton area and focus on the Miami Valley and small-town Ohio, but we can work with businesses anywhere by email and video call.
