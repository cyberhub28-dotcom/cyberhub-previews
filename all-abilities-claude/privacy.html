Privacy

How this site handles your information.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form on this site: your name, email and your message. We use it to reply to you and run club programs. Athlete registration happens on the club's Google Form, and gifts are made on Zeffy. Those services' own privacy policies also apply.

Children

A parent, guardian or adult volunteer fills in every form. Forms on this site don't ask for details about children. Please don't send medical records through the site.

Donations

Gifts are made on the club's Zeffy page. Zeffy's own privacy policy covers what you enter there.

What we don't do

We don't sell personal information, and this site doesn't run advertising cookies.

Email

The volunteer and contact forms open your own email app with your message filled in. Nothing is sent until you press Send there.

Facebook

Links to the club's Facebook page open Facebook, and Facebook's own privacy policy applies there. This site doesn't load Facebook on its own pages.
