Why we started

It started with a team for Bennett.

As parents to a child with autism, we understand firsthand how difficult it can be to find recreational opportunities where children with different abilities feel truly welcomed, supported, and included. What began as a desire to create a place where our son Bennett could create the same friendships our older daughter experiences within her club sports has grown into a mission to provide those same opportunities to other families in our community.

Amber Davidson, Founder

Ask a question
What is Winter Indoor Soccer?

An indoor soccer season for children of all abilities. It runs six Sundays, from January 10 to February 14, 2027.

When does registration open and close?

Registration opens November 20, 2026. It closes December 18, 2026, or sooner if all 48 spots are filled.

How do I register my child?

A parent or guardian registers each athlete on the club's online registration form. The link goes live on the sign-up page when registration opens.

How much does it cost?

The registration fee is $30 per athlete.

What ages can play?

Winter Indoor Soccer starts at age 3. If your child is a little younger and ready to join in, they're welcome too. To confirm: top age

Does my child need soccer experience?

No. No experience is needed.

Can I help without signing up an athlete?

Yes. You can volunteer as a coach, a buddy or an event-day helper, or support the club with a gift.

Who do I contact with a question?

Email Amber Davidson, the club's founder, at amber.davidson@allabilitiessportsclub.com.
