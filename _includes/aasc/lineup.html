{%- comment -%} Sorts the sports (one file each in _aasc_sports, edited in Pages CMS > Sports) by status. active_sports = Open now sports, then Opening soon ones, each soonest sign-up date first. main = the first active sport (it drives the Home page countdown, scoreboard and season questions). later_sports = Coming later, over_sports = Season over, both A to Z. Hidden ones are left out.
