Before you buy.

  • No account or server. Your data stays on your device only — CyberHub can’t see or restore it.
  • iPhone: data in a Safari tab can be wiped after about 7 days. Add to Home Screen — it’s safe from clearing history.
  • Android: the Home Screen app shares Chrome storage — clearing cookies/site data wipes it.
  • Moving from a tab to Home Screen: your data doesn’t carry over unless you Backup, then Restore.
  • Backup saves one unencrypted file. You own and keep your backups private. The app shows a backup reminder when you open it if you haven't backed up in a week.
  • Big photos/PDFs make a bigger backup file.
  • Credentials Tracker tracks only the dates/hours you enter — no board filing, no license verification, no clinical or legal advice.
  • Wedding Command Center and Small Business Toolkit work the same way — on-device, no account. Small Business pricing tools are estimates you enter, not tax, legal or accounting advice.
